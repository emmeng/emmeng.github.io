Overview

A train to somewhere quieter

1:AM Train is a reimagination of my previous web project, Midnight Train, in VR. Users board as a ghost and ride a subway traveling through infinite space with three distinct station stops, each designed around a different mode of decompression. The spatial freedom of VR transforms flat menu-driven features in the web version into physical places users can be immersed in.

VR deepens interactions beyond a 2D screen

Presence changes behavior. VR decreases the psychological distance of decompression because body involvement makes the interaction feel more meaningful. Unlike a screen, it also removes outside distractions entirely, giving users the mental space to actually unwind.

Technical

System Highlights

Voice journaling
Hand free interactions

Typing in VR requires complex cognitive attention and dexterity that interrupts the emotions users are trying to process in the journaling experience. Voice input allows users to speak continuously without breaking focus. The implementation samples microphone waveform data each frame to drive a real-time visual level indicator, giving user confirmation of their input.

Swimming locomotion
OpenXR

For our station Sea Eleven, swimming is the primary locomotion method in place of teleportation. Each frame, controller velocity and direction are sampled and translated into directional movement forces, mimicking the resistance and flow of underwater motion.


Shaders & VFX
Unity ShaderGraph

Each station has its own visual atmosphere, built with a combination of custom shaders and particle effects. I created reusable unlit gradient skyboxes with procedurally generated stars to avoid texture assets. I also created seamless scrolling textures across various environmental elements to add liveliness to the scene.


For smaller environmental details, I used particle systems with alpha blended textures to create lightweight environmental effects.

Features

Three stations with different focuses

Metro Dawn
Passive rest

Metro Dawn is the home station and entry point into our application. Users are greeted by the conductor who sets the tone, reminding them to slow down from the fast pace of everyday life.


Once users board the train, they have access the subway map to navigate between stations. The wrist menu, accessible by tapping the virtual watch, surfaces the daily mood tracker, voice journal, settings, help, and exit. The journal accepts open-ended recordings or generates a random prompt if the user needs a starting point. Users can also sit on the train benches and watch drifting clouds and shooting stars pass by, as it’s a moment designed for rest.

Sea Eleven
Active movement

Sea Eleven is an underwater environment designed for decompression through active movement. Research suggests blue space environments, even virtual ones, naturally reduce stress through ambient sound, muted lighting and the sensory qualities of water.


Users physically swim to navigate, creating a gentle physical rhythm that grounds attention in the present moment. Interactions are layered by object type, haptic feedback for marine life and distinct visual and audio cues for plants.

Golden Aster
Social

Golden Aster is an open sky environment designed for introspection. The station features a wishmaking interaction, inviting users to pause and reflect beneath an open sky. The reflective ground surface is suppose to reinforce this inward headspace to also create a sense of stillness.

While the vision for this station includes anonymous multiplayer conversations, the current prototype features this as a single-user experience due to development time constraints.

Reflection

Takeaways from transitioning web to VR

Benefits
  • Headsets create psychological boundary that signals to a brain that the active time is intentional and separate
  • Spatial audio, haptic feedback, and 360 environments all engage the nervous system in ways a flat screen cannot
Tradeoffs
  • Headsets exclude users with motion sickness, physical limitations, or no access to hardware
  • VR requires deliberate effort to set up and put on, which can work against an easy, repeatable, wellness habit

Why It Exists

The Gap in Mental Health Support

Midnight Train was built in response to a growing problem since the pandemic. Mental health struggles, especially among youth have been accelerating since Covid. The goal was to create a a comforting space with practical wellness tools that fit naturally into everyday life.

People worldwide affected by depression (WHO)

Research basis

Giving space for personal reflection without judgment has been linked to higher stress tolerance, personal growth, and emotional stability. Social interaction also relieves stress. The app was designed to support both individual reflection through journaling, and optional social connection through anonymous chat.

Technical

System Highlights

Anonymous Chat
Socket.io

I implemented the real time chat using Socket.io's event based client server communication. Each connected user is associated with their socket ID on the server, which allows messages and connection events to be broadcast to the other users in the room. Chat identity is kept separate from Firebase authentication, so users appear as anonymous regardless of whether they are logged in.

Train Environment Interactions
Custom 2D Positional & Collision Logic

The train environment uses our own 2D positional and collision logic to define the playable space. Because the train is in an isometric perspective, its angled walls and boundaries were translated into mathematical line equations, with positional checks performed each time the player moved to make sure movement is still within the train.


Additional positional regions were defined for fixed areas such as poles and seats. These checks prevent the player from moving through obstacles while also notifying the player of interaction zones. For example, entering the seat region enables the sit interaction, while entering the conductor’s region enables the conductor interaction.


Because the train was designed around a consistent coordinate space, the visual environment and collision geometry remain aligned as the scene scales to different screen sizes.

Features

Website Features

Persistent data with Accounts
Firebase Authentication

Users can sign in via Firebase Authentication, which ties journal entries and preferences to their account across sessions and devices. Guest access is still supported allowing unauthenticated users to access anonymous chat and the breathing exercise.

Interactive environment
Visual Effects

Different features of the train are interactable. Clicking wall posters generates random uplifting quotes. The conductor has multiple full dialogue sequence triggered by approaching the door. Users can also sit on the bench to watch the stars.

Anonymous chat
Socket.io bidirectional chat

All users connect to a single shared room and appear anonymously as they are more likely to say how they feel.

Journaling
Firestore

Users can write and save daily entries stored in Firestore which is linked to their account. Entries are private and guided prompts are available for users who aren't sure what to write.

Spotify Integration
Spotify API

Users can connect their Spotify account and listen to their own custom playlists rather than a fixed BGM lo-fi track to make their own "space".

Breathing exercise
Guided sessions

User-guided breathing sessions with timed inhale/hold/exhale prompts to help users relax and focus.
