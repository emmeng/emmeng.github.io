Mentor Reality

An AR language learning world exploration game meant to motivate users through one of the most tedious parts of language learning — memorizing vocabulary. Players are introduced to the ICRC, the Interplanetary Communications Research Company whose aim is to solve language barrier problems by studying how human brains memorize words. Dr. Zero, their boss, offers you a job to help them!
